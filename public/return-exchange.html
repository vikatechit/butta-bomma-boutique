Return & Exchange Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 • Verified Customer Care Policy
📥 Download PDF

1. 7-Day Hassle-Free Exchange & Return Window

We are dedicated to your complete satisfaction with our heirloom weaves. If your piece arrives with a manufacturing defect, fabric flaw, transit damage, or size discrepancy, you may initiate an exchange or return request within 7 calendar days of parcel delivery.

2. Return Eligibility Criteria

  • The piece must be completely unworn, unwashed, unaltered, and free of perfumes or makeup marks.
  • Original luxury packaging, authenticity silk tags, and invoice must be preserved.
  • Unboxing Video: For claims regarding missing items or physical transit damage, a short continuous unboxing video recorded while opening the courier package is required.

3. Custom Stitched & Bespoke Tailoring Pieces

Items custom tailored to personalized measurements (e.g. stitched blouses, fall-pico finished sarees, customized lehenga skirts) are crafted exclusively for you. These pieces are eligible for complimentary master alterations to ensure a flawless fit, rather than full refunds.

4. Simple Return Process

  1. Contact our WhatsApp Concierge at +91 73828 91980 with your Order ID and photos of the piece.
  2. Our team will arrange a reverse pickup directly from your doorstep via our courier network.
  3. Once the item passes inspection at our boutique atelier, your exchange piece or refund is initiated within 3 business days.

5. Refund Timelines

Prepaid orders are refunded to the original payment source (UPI / Bank Account) within 3 to 5 business days. For COD orders, refunds are credited directly to your bank account via UPI / NEFT upon receipt of account details.
