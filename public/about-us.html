About Butta Bomma Collections

Effective Date: September 2026 • Verified Brand Heritage Document
📥 Download PDF

1. The Butta Bomma Heritage

Born out of deep reverence for India's rich artisanal heritage, Butta Bomma Collections weaves timeless handloom traditions into contemporary royal silhouettes. Every motif, zari brocade, and seam is crafted with uncompromising mastery to bring you authentic celebratory couture.

2. Generational Craftsmanship & Ethical Fair Trade

We work directly with generational master weavers across India's most celebrated textile clusters—including Kanchipuram, Banaras, Chanderi, and Surat. By partnering directly with artisan families and cooperatives, we ensure fair compensation, preservation of ancient loom techniques, and pure unadulterated fabrics for our patrons worldwide.

3. Curated festive & Bridal Collections

Our boutique features a distinguished selection of pure Katan silk sarees, Kanjivaram celebration weaves, zardozi bridal lehengas, scalloped organza anarkalis, and handcrafted cotton kurtis. Each piece undergoes multi-stage quality inspections to guarantee flawlessness in weave, border alignment, and embroidery.

4. Bespoke Custom Tailoring & Fit Guarantee

We believe every woman deserves a bespoke fit that celebrates her silhouette. Our in-house master tailors provide custom blouse stitching, fall-pico edging, custom size adjustments from XS to 3XL, and custom neckline styling with concierge WhatsApp consultations.
