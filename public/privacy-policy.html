1. Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Butta Bomma Collections, your privacy and trust are of the utmost importance. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, and safeguard personal information when you purchase from our boutique or interact with our concierge.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact Information: Customer Name, WhatsApp Mobile Number, Delivery Address, City, Pincode.
  • Order & Tailoring Data: Chosen sizes, customized blouse measurements, and order history.
  • Financial Information: We do NOT collect or store full debit/credit card numbers or banking passwords on our servers. All UPI transactions are processed through secure bank gateways and verified merchant channels.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To fulfill your order, arrange door-to-door courier dispatch, and send tracking notifications.
  • To assist you with custom measurements and sizing guidance over WhatsApp.
  • To enable private order lookup where you can check your order status using only your registered mobile number.

4. Information Sharing & Security

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information to third-party marketing companies. Customer delivery details are shared strictly with our verified express courier partners (e.g., Blue Dart, Delhivery) for fulfillment purposes only.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain transaction and delivery records for accounting and warranty verification. You may request deletion or correction of your personal contact records by contacting our Data Grievance Officer.
